SUN wordmark: four directions

All four use Inter Tight as the base so the mark stays in the same family as the site. Each is shown at hero size, at nav size beside the legal name, and reversed. Switch your system theme to check both grounds.

A. Tight Black

Inter Tight 800, tracking −0.085em. This is what the site uses today, so it is the baseline. Dense, confident, reads as a firm stamp. Risk: at 320px the letters nearly touch.

B. Open Caps

Inter Tight 500, tracking +0.32em. Opens the letters up and drops the weight. Feels like a name on a building, closer to a traditional law firm. Scales down without crowding the theme button on phones.

C. Sun Dot

Inter Tight 700 lowercase, with a solid gold disc as a full stop. The sun is the only color in the mark, so it can be dropped into the Locations cards and favicon on its own. Friendlier, less formal.

D. Horizon

Inter Tight 800 caps with the U's counter filled by a half disc, so the letter reads as a horizon with the sun rising in it. The most ownable of the four and the one that needs a drawn U, so it should be built as an SVG with outlined letters before it ships.

The half disc position is a rough placement over the live text. A final version needs the U outlined and the disc centered on its counter.

Recommendation

  • Start with B or D. B is the safest fit beside the legal name; D is the one people will remember.
  • A and C stay in the pool only if the firm wants a louder or warmer tone.
  • Whichever wins, hand Cole one SVG plus a favicon crop, so he swaps one asset and does not touch the responsive work.
