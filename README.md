# SUN

### Sun, Kim, Diamond & Goldman LLP

**Review / Publish**  
[Live GitHub Pages site](https://sukonik.github.io/GoldenSunLaw/) · [Homepage source: index.html](./index.html)

**GoldenSunLaw** is a public reference implementation and reusable template for a premium international law firm website.

SUN is a fictional global law firm created to explore how a modern legal brand can combine the institutional confidence of traditional BigLaw with a responsive, technology-forward digital experience.

> **Fictional demonstration notice**
>
> SUN, Sun, Kim, Diamond & Goldman LLP, Sun & Kim LLP, Diamond & Goldman LLP, their attorneys, matters, offices, and client relationships are fictional and created solely for this design and development project. References to real companies, properties, and institutions are illustrative only and do not imply an actual attorney-client relationship, endorsement, affiliation, or representation.

## Current Design System

- **Default theme:** Hudson Cobalt
- **Headline type:** Inter Tight

- **Theme control:** persistent `Change Theme` picker with Hudson Cobalt, Pacific Sapphire, Midnight Indigo, Emerald, and Graphite

## Brand

The public-facing identity is deliberately simple:

# SUN

The full formal identity is:

**Sun, Kim, Diamond & Goldman LLP**

- **Public brand:** SUN
- **Formal legal entity:** Sun, Kim, Diamond & Goldman LLP
- **Positioning:** Technology. Real Estate. Capital. Infrastructure.
- **Core thesis:** **Technology needs land.**

SUN is designed to feel established, international, precise, editorial, technologically fluent, and restrained.

The brand should avoid familiar small-firm visual clichés—gavels, columns, scales, handshakes, generic skylines, and gold-gradient “luxury” treatments. The visual language instead centers on typography, architecture, people, transactions, cities, infrastructure, and ideas.

- Semiconductor facilities
- Communications towers
- Fiber networks
- Energy systems
- Offices
- Resorts and hotels
- Retail environments

- Physical devices

At the same time, modern real estate increasingly depends on software, sensors, data governance, automation, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence.

Sun & Kim understands the technology.

Diamond & Goldman understands the assets.

**SUN understands the system.**

## Responsive & Bilingual Architecture

The site should be mobile-first and fully responsive.

### Desktop
Large editorial grids, generous white space, strong typography, multi-column matter and insight layouts.

### Tablet
Adaptive two-column compositions and reduced navigation complexity.

### Mobile
Single-column editorial stream, large touch targets, coherent navigation, and no arbitrary disappearance of important desktop content.

Bilingual support should be designed from the beginning:

**EN | 한국어**

The English and Korean experiences should share the same structure, hierarchy, and visual system.

## Implemented Site Foundation

The first website build uses a lightweight static architecture:

- Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- No framework or package-install requirement
- GitHub Pages friendly
- Mobile-first responsive layout
- Accessible skip links, focus states, and mobile navigation
- Reduced-motion support
- Shared design tokens and reusable card/grid patterns
- A firm-profile switch for enterprise vs. solo/small-firm deployments

### Small-Firm Mode

Open `js/site-config.js` and change:

```js
profile: "enterprise"
```

to:

```js
profile: "small"
```

Small-firm mode removes enterprise-only navigation and sections, changes selected copy, and allows lawyer/practice/office grids to flow naturally for one or two attorneys.

For a solo or two-lawyer deployment:

1. Keep only the lawyer cards actually needed in `people.html`.
2. Keep only the firm's real practice areas in `practices.html`.
3. Replace the SUN brand values in `js/site-config.js`.
4. Replace contact details and office copy in `about.html`.
5. Leave Deals & Cases disabled unless the firm has appropriate public matters to feature.
6. Add jurisdiction-specific attorney-advertising and professional-responsibility language before production use.

The design intentionally remains credible without photography, so a small practice can launch a polished version before commissioning custom portraits or office imagery.
